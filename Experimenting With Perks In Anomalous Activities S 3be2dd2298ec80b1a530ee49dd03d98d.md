I was playing Anomalous Activities: Sierra Protocol on Roblox where I was experimenting with the trade-offs of fictional perks.

The forcefield perk absorbed one hit from the monster. I confronted the monster but I was quickly killed after being hit twice so it felt close to useless.

The invisibility perk granted 5 seconds of invisibility and a speed boost to escape the monster but had a 1 minute cooldown. I was crouching on top of the metal shelves in a small rectangular room waiting for the cooldown to end when the monster entered through the doorway and noticed me. I moved to the back of the shelf as the last seconds ticked down. I turned invisible and fled down the hallway into the room on the right. However, the monster figured out where I went and when I turned around I saw it use a sprint perk and charge into the room to insta kill me.
