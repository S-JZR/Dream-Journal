I was spectating my unit complex when a severe storm moved past during the night with strong winds and rain. After the storm passed, a white van parked in front of my unit on the sidewalk and released a white weather balloon with blue LEDs covering the entire bottom into the sky that was anchored to the ground and drove off. I walked to the same spot to meet my dad and brother who were already standing there. I looked up and was surprised to notice the weather balloon swaying in the wind.

> **Me**: There is a weather balloon up there.
> 

I used a compact digital camera to record zooming into the balloon to get a better look at it. I played back the footage to discover there were strange noises that weren’t there before but reasoned it was merely the sound of the camera zooming in. The initial footage was blurry because it was shaky but it eventually showed a closeup of the LED’s on the balloon which displayed the GUI settings of a camera which I thought was strange. My dad wanted to find out where they came from so he drove to an amusement park to see if they had any of the weather balloons but they didn’t.

Three male private investigators wearing black tuxedos and dark sunglasses appeared on the sidewalk and I hired them to investigate the balloon and they reported back to me. I never heard them directly and shared their responses with my family who were in the lounge room.

> **Me**: I hired some private investigators to tell me what they thought the hot hair balloon was but they weren't as helpful as I hoped. The first said it was from a billion dollar company, the second said it's a black hole, and the third said it was a UFO.
