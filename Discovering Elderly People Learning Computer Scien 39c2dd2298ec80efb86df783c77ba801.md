I was at a shopping centre when I overheard a loud male voice speaking in a room and entered inside to see various elderly people with grey hair learning about computer science. They were seated at desks in a classroom eagerly writing notes on books as they watched a lecture video about databases from my university on a TV cart at the front of the room. I thought to myself that given their old age, the current state of the market with AI making it difficult to get entry level software development jobs, and how poor my learning experience was that they had no chance of getting anywhere with it. I turned around and saw a multi-level brochure holder at the back of the room with three pages featuring the average star reviews of the course by students that ranged from 10/10, 6.3/10, and 6.0/10. I thought to myself that I must not be the only one who realizes there are problems.
