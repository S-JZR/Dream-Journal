I was spectating various children and teenagers seated on a white bus being driven through a mall into a large candy store at night during Christmas week as they looked for opportunities to work for extra money. The store featured a mostly black interior with a carpet, silk on tables, and lots of aisles.

After dismounting the bus everyone realized all of the hard work they had to do and abruptly left except two black children and a teenager from the same family. The male store owner approached them.

> **Man**: I never shut my door. They chose to walk away. *Chuckle*. So why did you stay?
> 

> **Teenager**: We want to earn extra money for our family. We're prepared to stay the whole night. We've told our parents and they're aware of it.
> 

> **Man**: I'll tell you what. If you work the whole shift, I'll even let you each pick one product to take home for free.
>
