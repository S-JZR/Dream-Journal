I was attending a YouTube Award Ceremony with my family at a large 2-storey facility. The front of the facility featured a golden fence, a diagonal red carpet and pool strip leading to a part of the facility on the right and a diagonal footpath parallel to the carpet leading to a part of the facility on the left. I was walking along the footpath to the left as various celebrities were walking along the carpet to the right. Various male employees were unloading custom made floating pool chairs onto the pool strip and pushed them along as they bunched together. A man walking in front of us was mentioning their names.

> **Man 1**: Katy Perry … Elon Musk.
> 

> **Me**: (To mum) (whisper) Elon must is here?!
> 

In the indoor pool with a brick floor and skylights people were floating around in inflatable doughnuts.

In the men’s restroom, I entered a toilet stall and felt compelled to check if someone was spying on me. I stood on top of the toilet to look over the top of the stall and saw the head of a man 1 stall away popping up at the same time and looking at me.

> **Man 2**: Ahh!
> 

> **Me**: Ahh!
> 

I descended and he exited his stall and stood in front of mine to talk with me.

> **Me**: What made you look over the stall?
> 

> **Man 2**: I just felt to check. When I looked over the stall I initially thought that I was looking at my reflection in a mirror then I realized it was somebody else which scared me.
> 

I instinctively felt that we both had the same synergy which would make us good friends but he left and nothing came of it.

In the theatre room, there was a wooden stage and columns of fancy red chairs with gold fringes with a red carpet between them leading to back doors.

In the apartment on the second floor, I encountered two men sitting at desks with computers, a door to the right leading to a pitch-black bedroom, and a glass balcony overlooking a hallway and a glass wall with a white symmetrical minimalist commercial kitchen featuring:

- perimeter: long rectangular benches with gaps between them.
- centre: a multi-purpose cooking area comprising 1 horizontal rectangular bench on the ends and 2 vertical rectangular benches in the centre. Each bench had translucent perimeter guards and a rectangular pad with two modes a smooth surface and a dark grey grill. The pads changed mode by descending, flipping, and ascending.
- Food was was laid on top of the benches including salmon on ice.

Gordan Ramsay was preparing dishes by himself. I wondered what would happen if someone left food on the pads and they fell beneath them.

A blond woman was messing with the controls on the central bench closet to me while Gordan Ramsay was standing in front of it and somehow fell inside. He was submerged up to his head in grease and trapped inside.

> **Gordan Ramsay**: Just wait for it to flip around and do it again.
> 

I briefly walked away when I overheard the woman screaming and crying distraughtly.

> **Woman**: I killed Gordan Ramsay! I killed Gordan Ramsay!
> 

I sprinted downstairs into the theatre as she was running around and screaming. I decided to hold her down on the ground so she wouldn’t cause a scene everywhere and I left her with other people who came to investigate. I returned to the apartment.

> **Me**: Did you hear what happened?
> 

> **Man 3**: We looked into it online and discovered that she organized it as a sick prank to boost her popularity.
