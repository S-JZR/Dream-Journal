I was spectating two aliens that looked like girls in my year five classrooms at primary school when they started a business selling small pizzas that they cooked in ovens. The popularity of the pizzas spread by word of mouth but their father was furious and rang them on their phone.

> **Dad**: No daughter of mine is going to stoop to selling pizzas to humans. I’m coming to pick you up.
> 

> **Girl 1**: We need to move now otherwise we will get caught.
> 

> **Girl 2**: He has GPS and can easily track us anywhere in the world.
> 

> **Girl 1**: I will distract him. It might buy us some time.
> 

One of the girls sprinted down the left road in the T-junction outside the school. She glanced over her shoulder to notice that her father, a grey alien, driving a mini pink spaceship with a pill-shaped canopy that levitated above the road was pursuing her in the distance and rapidly catching up.

> **Girl 1**: I’m going to hide in the bushland and teleport back.
> 

> **Girl 2**: Ok.
> 

The girl fled into the bushland to the left of the road and pressed a big red button on a small dark-grey controller with an antenna. After waiting 10 seconds and standing completely still she was teleported back to the classroom. The girls fled into the bushland behind the school.

> **Girl 1**: Let’s flee into the bushland behind the school where it will be difficult for him to find us.
>
