I was ascending escalators from the underground car park in a shopping mall and entered a large room with an audience seated in front of a stage with two chairs on it. A black woman and I sat on the stage and faced each other for a discussion.

> **Woman**: How can humanity find peace moving forward?
> 

> **Me**: Those who love the law will find peace.
> 

> **Crowd**: *Applause*.
