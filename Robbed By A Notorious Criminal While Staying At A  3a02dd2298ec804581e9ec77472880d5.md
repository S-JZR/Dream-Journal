I was with my family in a remote desert town without roads when we decided to stay a themed two-storey motel with cars containing beds half-protruding from the top floors. My family entered the room furthest to the left on the top floor and saw a large opaque rectangular plastic container covering most of the centre of the room full of miscellaneous fictional personal belongings.

I noticed a $2 gold coin inside and picked it up to see the face of a man which I wondered if it was rare. When we walked back through the hallway, I saw a coin store built into the wall with a large white banner ad on top encouraging people to see if their coins were valuable and there was a large picture of my coin on it.

> **Me**: Look, my coin is on the banner so it must be valuable.
> 

> **Mum**: Ok.
> 

While waiting on the other side of the hallway a skinny Mexican man with a moustache wearing a red flower Hawaiian shirt, jeans, and a domino mask riding a motorbike emerged from stairs and stopped in front of our door. I instinctively knew he was a renowned criminal nicknamed El Heartio because he left the imagery of hearts at the scenes of crimes like cards and spray paint on walls. I saw an image of El Macho from Despicable Me 2. He revved his motorbike and rammed down the door to get inside. He loaded most of our possessions in the car and drove it onto the road to escape.

> **Me**: I think we’re being robbed.
> 

I wasn’t sure if our room was being blatantly robbed while we were standing nearby and I leaned over to take a look. I pulled out my phone and called the police but I started to forget what he looked like.

> **Woman**: What is your emergency?
> 

> **Me**: [Relays the situation].
> 

> Woman: What was he wearing?
> 

> Me: A red flower Hawaiian shirt, jeans, and a… black tie.
> 

We moved to another motel during the same night for our safety but El Heartio robbed us again.

I entered the top floor of a two-storey shopping mall and walked past a makeshift storage shed as workers were constructing something in an area enclosed with a chain-link fence. After overhearing a metal object crashing onto the floor, the workers and I turned around to see the shed door open with empty metal shelves inside.

> **Man**: Someone has stolen our equipment!
