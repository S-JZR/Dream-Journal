I was standing with Dejan, my friend from secondary school, on a floor filled with employees working in office cubicles in a skyscraper bank we were about to rob. The bank was owned by Frieda Pilkington, the villain from Animal Farm. Dejan tapped a button on a hacking app on his phone which locked all of the computers with ransomware that displayed a white screen with a red 5 minute countdown until the system was destroyed and a phone number.

> **Me**: Where did you get that app from?
> 

> **Dejan**: I got it from hackers who have 15 years of experience.
> 

The bank owner rang him.

> **Frieda**: It’s preposterous to think that I would ever pay you anything.
> 

When the countdown ended, Dejan hesitated and wondered whether we should go through with it but when fiddling with the app he accidentally pressed a button that caused a blackout.

> **Dejan**: I didn’t mean to do that.
> 

We went downstairs to a gold vault with a circular door and opened it to reveal a small yellow rectangular room with gold bars stacked across the floor. After stashing the bars in multiple black duffle bags we fled up the stairwell to the helicopter pads on the roof.

> **Me**: Did you book the helicopters yet?
> 

> **Dejan**: …
> 

> **Me**: You better do it quick so we can get out of here!
> 

While running up the stairs, Dejan opened the first helicopter chauffeur company website he found online and frantically tapped through the form to order three helicopters for immediate pickup without worrying about the cost. A male dispatcher rang him for a security check and he improvised.

> **Man**: I noticed you made a booking on short notice.
> 

> **Man**: Can I ask what this is for?
> 

> **Dejan**: We are travelling to a special birthday party event held by our company.
> 

> **Man**: Alright, I’ll send them over right away.
> 

> **Man**: Enjoy the celebration.
> 

We waited on the roof until the helicopters landed, loaded the duffle bags onboard, and flew away.
