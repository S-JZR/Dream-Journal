I was building a tiny saucer UFO in the carpark of my unit complex when a small saucer UFO emerged and hovered above me. I dragged my build onto the doormat at my front door and the UFO moved above me again. I went inside and shut the door for my protection. After reopening the door I noticed that the UFO was gone and it exchanged my build for a brown paper bag filled with food. I picked up the bag which felt warm on the bottom and placed it on the oven where my mum was standing and opened it to see small cardboard food boxes and fries on top. I took a bite of a fry.

> **Me**: I probably shouldn’t be eating this in case the aliens did something to it.
> 

> **Mum**: Yes.
> 

I called the police to report the incident and they dispatched a male officer to investigate.

> **Me**: [Relaying the situation]. I have a phone tracker app with a map that shows it’s in Malaysia. There is an icon above it with the photo of a woman and a business name.
> 

We flew to the business in Malaysia which was a small office beside a carpark with a grey brick wall to the left with a black fence on top.

> **Man**: This is a witness protection program phone service run by Mrs Mildred, the woman you saw, and her husband Mr Arthur.
> 

We were walking on the outside of the wall when the man pulled off a large rectangular cardboard advertisement sign from the business and handed it to me.

> **Man**: You can purchase one of these phone plans to redirect calls to maintain your privacy when the public hears the news.
> 

> **Me**: Sure I will consider that.
> 

> **Man**: Mrs Mildred is a baddie. When you ring them, ask to talk with Mr Arthur.
> 

We never solved the incident and the office was shut.
