I was at the industrial design block in secondary school when I noticed Lisa from the Simpsons seated inside at a wooden bench sketching isolated images of drones in square panels scattered across an A3 page. She was adding obvious callouts like "fly" and "propellers”. I knew she was not being strategic and confronted her about it.

> **Me**: You’re not approaching the design process properly. You’re wasting your effort drawing these images without putting them to good use. You should ground the vision by describing the specific characteristics and functionality that you want in the design and create a storyboard that explains the use case.
> 

I drew an arrow showing airflow beneath a propeller.

> **Me**: For example, you could explain why the propeller needs a specific shape to create lift.
> 

> **Lisa**: Ok, thanks.
