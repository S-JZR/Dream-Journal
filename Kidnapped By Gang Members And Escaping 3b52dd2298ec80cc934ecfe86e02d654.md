I was standing in the city with people nearby including average people, an obese woman, and a morbidly obese woman. I noticed a male gang member's life was in danger as a bucket full of concrete falling from a tall building was about to land on his head so I pulled him to safety. The gang leader and other members emerged in a silver van and proceeded to kidnap the other people and me as we were forced to sit inside and bags were placed onto our heads so we couldn’t see where we were going. I overheard the man yelling at his leader who ignored him.

> **Man**: Please spare this man, he saved my life!
> 

I instinctively knew that the gang leader was ruthless and plotting to kill all of us. We arrived at my unit complex and while walking inside my unit I noticed there was a pile of kitchen knives in the front garden to one side shimmering in the sunlight so I leaned down to hide one under my shirt which nobody saw me doing. Our bags were removed from our heads and the other hostages were taken upstairs in my room but I stayed downstairs in the lounge room with an armed male guard. When we faced the wall and nobody else was around, I exploited the opportunity to stab him in the back. As he turned around to fight back I kept stabbing him in the chest and he collapsed. The other hostages fled with me over the back fence until we encountered a large rectangular concrete area with a two-storey shaft. We descended a metal pipe to the bottom and fled through a large sewer pipe.
