I was standing in the toy section of Big W at my local supermarket where I took a flamethrower toy off the shelf and a horde of zombies emerged from the distance. The flamethrower was a transparent white rounded plastic cylinder with a detachable funnel on the front. I fled all the way back to my unit and was chased by them up the stairs. When I got into my room I turned around and sprayed a stream of flames to ignite them all. I shifted my focus to the toy and all of the zombies disappeared.

I experimented with the flamethrower by dethatching the funnel which made it shoot long yellow flames and attached the funnel which made it shoot short blue flames. I touched the flames and was confused to discover they were freezing and figured that they were an illusion projected onto mist that it sprayed into the air.
