I was playing The Conquerors 3 in a 8 vs 8 gamemode on a symmetrical mountainous map with two open ovals on both sides connected by a narrow S-shape pathway. Since most players left it was a 1 vs 2. I spammed power plants on my side to quickly establish a basic economy. The enemy entered into my side so I built a barracks to make 8 heavy soldiers and 2 snipers. I initially had the advantage and was able to push back the enemy team but the map turned into a 50:50 stalemate.

I attempted to seal the entrance to my side with a fort and barracks. However, the enemy team upgraded to nuclear power plants which gave them about an extra $35 a minute and researched juggernauts that destroyed my frontline base. I realized that I messed up by being too nice instead of merciless and focusing entirely on fighting instead of my economy but nothing could be done since we were already past the tipping point. I raged quit and pushed over furniture in my room which everyone heard on the microphone and laughed at (I would never do that in real life).

I tried to join another server but realized it was the same one but now I assumed the role of orange, a team member who left earlier. I noticed they had built all nuclear power plants and had $1400 which I could use.
