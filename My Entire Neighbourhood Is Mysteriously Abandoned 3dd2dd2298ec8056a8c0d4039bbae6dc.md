I was seated in the passenger seat of my mum’s car with my family being driven by my mum to our unit complex. The atmosphere had a grey haze and everything had muted colours. We knew something was wrong because everyone was gone and some units had their front wooden and fly screen doors left open.

When we arrived, we briefly got out to investigate. I looked into my unit from outside but was afraid to enter. We were unsure of what was happening and whether there was some kind of disaster or monsters we should be wary of.

> **Me**: We should get out of here in case we're in danger.
> 

We quickly returned to the car and drove off.
