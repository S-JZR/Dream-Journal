I was spectating a daredevil man holding a handheld camera filming two of his friends walking across a grassy plains to a remote farm house. A red channel 7 news helicopter was flying at a high altitude when a strong storm sent it spiralling out of control and it nose dived towards the house where it’s main blades got tangled in powerlines and dangled 10 metres off the ground. The man went almost directly beneath the helicopter and filmed the pilot and passenger inside who were terrified.

> **Woman**: Stop standing under the helicopter, it could fall on you!
> 

> **Man**: It’s fine, don’t worry about it.
> 

The helicopter crew released a rope from the back that fell to the ground and the man climbed up without any safety support.

> **Female Pilot**: You need to use the key to deploy all of the emergency backup systems.
> 

The man took a black key out of the back of the helicopter and turned it in a cylinder component at the bottom that opened to deployed a mini parachute that did nothing. He opened the next one that deployed many large parachutes which caught the wind and lifted the helicopter back to high altitude where it broke free.

The news covered the event and congratulated the man’s heroic acts.
