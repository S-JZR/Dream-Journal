I was seated in an inflatable dark green raft with a fictional Asian friend in the centre of a crowded lagoon. After paddling around for a few minutes, our raft started to sink and I tried to get the attention of those nearby.

> **Me**: Help! Our raft is sinking!
> 

My friend dived into the water and swam towards the shore but disappeared underwater. I never saw him again and was afraid that he drowned. A nearby man in a red raft approached parallel to mine and bumped against my right side.

> **Man**: Climb over!
> 

I jumped aboard before being taken to the sandbank.

The entire lagoon instantly dried up and I couldn’t see the body of my Asian friend anywhere on the bottom.

> **Me**: Have you seen where my friend went?
> 

> **Man**: He went to a party.
> 

The man pulled out his phone from his back pant pocket and showed me social media posts of my friend celebrating with various people in a large apartment with large glass panels.

> **Me**: Why didn’t he bother telling me that he went to a party instead of leaving me to worry that he drowned?!
>
