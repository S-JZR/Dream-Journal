I was standing in an area enclosed with a black fence in a park with trees scattered around the outside. It was autumn and orange leaves covered the entire ground so I sought to shovel them into a pile and dig a hole in the process. I didn’t want to waste time moving the pile again so I built multiple small grey dump truck robots with features including a battery, GPS navigation, and a rectangular tray on top that lifted. In the future I planned to add modular attachments to the front of them including a sweeper that could fling the leaves into the tray. The robots travelled in a loop by waiting in a queue beside me to be filled, dumping their load outside the area, and returning.
