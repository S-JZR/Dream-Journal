I assumed the role of a white rabbit standing on a slanted hill being chased by Jayba, my former cat. I sprinted away until I reached an obstacle course in an open area that seemed familiar but I couldn’t recognise where it was from featuring:

- left: a dead end built into towering fantasy walls in a mountain that looked like the opening of a hallway but lead to the elevated end platform.
- right: a square-ring-shaped obstacle course comprising thick rectangular, thin rectangular, and square platforms positioned at different elevations with gaps between them.

I entered the dead end then returned to finish the obstacle course. I entered my bedroom where I hid on top of the bunk bed inside a small pile of clothes. I looked over the railings and saw Jayba walk into the room and failing to find me. Eventually, I made a noise to make myself known and she jumped up to find me.
