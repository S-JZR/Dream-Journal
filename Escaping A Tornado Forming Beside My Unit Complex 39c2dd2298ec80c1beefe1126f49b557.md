I was standing in the paddock beside my unit complex with my family when we were concerned about lots of heavy wind blowing past the trees near the road. A tornado began to form and I ditched my family to flee up the street for my own safety until I reached a shopping mall. I entered a hallway that featured three sound proof rooms on both sides that people could temporarily claim and write a message about what they were doing on a tablet inside that projected it on a screen at the front. A green and red LED on the door indicated their locked status and there was a peephole on the door.

I opened an unlocked room but there was an elderly man already inside who turned to look at me.

> Me: Sorry I didn’t realize you were in here.
> 

I saw a room labelled “religious debates” and looked through the peephole at a man seemingly talking to the front door and assumed he was looking at a tripod behind the door. I don’t remember anything he said.

I claimed an unlocked room for myself and wrote something silly for the label but I felt uncomfortable that anyone could look through the peephole at anytime without me knowing so I left. A black man standing in the hallway read my label and gave me a blank stare as I left the building.
