I was playing Team Fortress 2 as the red sniper in a tall empty apartment building overlooking brick buildings and a street at night. I was accompanied by another sniper. I walked onto a rusted metal balcony to the right and attempted to headshot a distant blue sniper but repeatedly missed. They noticed me and fired back an arrow using a Hunstman which nearly hit me but I stepped to the side. I noticed a blue Pyro holding a Powerjack moving along the footpath below towards the entrance of our building. I walked to the top of the stairwell and attempted to headshot the Pyro ascending but repeatedly missed. After they reached the top, the other sniper and I unloaded our SMG magazines at them. They still had a little health left and I got into a melee fight as we repeatedly took swings in the air until I killed them with my Kukri.
