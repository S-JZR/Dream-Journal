I was standing in my backyard of my unit under the clothes line covered in a blue tarp when I reached over to the hose against the brick wall but felt a single thread of a spider web and pulled back. I noticed a light pink spider resembling a squid with large black eyes and no tentacles sitting idly on webs further back. The hose instantly materialized in my hand as I fired a focused stream of water directly at the spider from a distance behind the clothes line to try knocking it off the web so I could clear the web away. However, it remained steadfast and merely vibrated against the water. The spider leaped 1 metre in a straight line onto a cord on the washing line and faced me. I was afraid that it was preparing to jump on me next and fled inside. While I was closing the fly screen door behind me it jumped through the wire mesh which sliced it into multiple chunks that landed mostly on the tiles and a rectangular modular carpet with a few pieces on the wooden door and walls. The mesh also had a stain from where it hit.

I walked into the lounge room where my mother was seated at the other side of the dining table and assumed everything was normal as she scolded me.

> **Mum**: I told you to get the dishes done already so I can…
> 

I staggered beside the cat scratcher and repeatedly almost fell down.

> **Mum**: What’s wrong?
> 

> **Me**: I’m shook. [Recalling the situation].
> 

We walked to the back door and I showed her everything but we got too close and I accidentally stood on a piece of the spider which was cold and squishy then stepped back. She got paper towel and spray from the kitchen and cleaned up the mess.
