I was spectating a YouTube video on a computer monitor with my dad showcasing a Minecraft Redstone build featuring a long line of modern short buildings with vegetation and pistons raising up 10 block walls and decorations in front of them. The camera tracked right along a long concrete path to the right and focused on the buildings.

> **Me**: That’s so much overengineering. What is the point of building all of that? It’s impractical for everyday use.
> 

> **Dad**: Be quiet, you always over explain everything.
> 

PewDiePie was building a strange structure out of white blocks in a grass plains with three other players nearby. The building resembled a table with 4 legs and a rectangle on top with walking space around the perimeter and plus-shaped hallways cut out of the bottom. While PewDiePie stood on the second level an invisible player holding an empty glass bottle and particle effects approached him.

> **PewDiePie**: Someone is here. Who is that?
> 

The invisible player began punching him, flew around the building, and used WorldEdit to repeatedly erase large chunks of the building until it completely disappeared.

> **PewDiePie**: No no noooo!
> 

PewDiePie and his fictional family were invited to an award ceremony in a gala hall with a stage room featuring glass walls and an audience of rich people. His aunt was in a wheelchair. PewDiePie waited outside the stage area with his family until being called in.

> **Man 1**: Uncle Alex won’t be coming because he unexpectedly died in his sleep. He said goodnight everyone then passed away.
> 

> **Woman**: Before we present the awards to tonight’s Minecraft build teams, we have a special guest. Some of you may have heard of this YouTube personality before, please welcome PewDiePie!
> 

> **Crowd**: *Applause*.
> 

Some of the audience members were snobs and turned around to look at them through the glass panels with disgusted expressions wondering why a random pleb was attending. As PewDiePie’s family moved toward the stage and pushed the wheelchair, the narrow rows made it difficult to steer without bumping into people’s legs and feet, so it was parked just before the stage. PewDiePie accepted the award and left.

After the award ceremony, PewDiePie approached a hostess standing on the outdoor stairs and tried to talk with her when a distant guest downstairs interrupted.

> **Man 2**: Hey! Girl! Are you deaf? I told you ten minutes ago to bring me my medication!
> 

> **Woman 2**: It’s best if you leave.
>
