I was having a false awakening where I starred at the doorway of my room while breathing slowly and loudly. When I breathed in fast enough it caused an invisible geometric shadow figure draped in fabric standing idly in the doorway to flicker into view. The figure was holding a long sword vertically in its left hand with an L-shaped arm pose. I was worried that this was a demon that was secretly harming me in my everyday life which I was unaware of. I tried talking to it but I felt deenergized (which I also felt when waking up) and could only mumble unintelligible phrases.

> **Me**: Who are you?
> 

> **Me**: What do you want?
> 

The figure didn’t respond and I started making random groaning noises to try getting my family’s attention to rescue me but nobody came.

I jolted awake.
