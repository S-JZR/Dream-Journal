I was walking home from secondary school along the edge of a fictional bridge that didn’t have a sidewalk, heading towards my neighbourhood. A man driving a car pulled onto the bridge in front of me.

> Me: Stop blocking the road!
> 

I continued walking and encountered various students from my primary school waiting to cross the 4 way intersection to the retail strip. I looked down and noticed that I was wearing the same primary school uniform and felt embarrassed about being seen by people driving by. I crouched down a little bit and kept moving around to blend in. The other students were about the height of teenagers so I didn’t need to crouch much. At the next set of crossing I was going to travel on the opposite side of the road in the direction of my home but a male student confronted me.

> **Boy**: Don’t you live on the hill over there? You should travel the other way or you will have to cross twice.
> 

> **Me**: Yeah, you’re right. I will do that now. Thanks.
> 

When walking away I felt concerned about how he knew where I lived without telling him. I walked up the hill to my unit complex and noticed a teenage woman wearing a black mask standing behind the wooden fence on the concrete pathway to the right of a garage. I started walking past her to the right but she moved to block the path so I travelled to the left and went around her. As I walked past the front of my unit she was travelling closely behind me so I turned around to draw a pistol on her and she pointed one at me.

> **Me**: Who are you and what are you doing here?
> 

> **Woman**: I bugged your house.
> 

She walked along the path and disappeared behind unit 6. I continuously faced her direction as I unlocked the front door, stepped inside, and locked the door. Her father was annoyed with me and approached the door as he forced his forehead onto the fly screen to intimidate me. The woman also stood to the left behind him. I shot him in the head with a light calibre pistol and a high calibre pistol but they did nothing.

> **Woman**: Dad don’t.
> 

> **Man**: *chuckle*
