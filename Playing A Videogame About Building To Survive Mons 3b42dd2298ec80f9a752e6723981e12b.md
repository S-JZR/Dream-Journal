I was in a survival videogame with a woman where we had to build to survive monsters with different abilities each night and used money to purchase structures. We progressed through multiple levels that grew in difficulty.

In a hard level, we were on a grassy plains at night with spread out houses that featured no lights and empty door frames and long horizontal rectangular window frames. There were roaming black monsters which we didn’t know the locations of and couldn’t look at them since they would become hostile and fire long spikes that would penetrate our skin and yank us towards them. The spikes could penetrate through wooden planks. We also had to defend a house without drawing attention to our location with loud noises. We placed metal spikes around the perimeter of a house as a passive deterrent. After getting inside we built ceiling lights and boarded up the door frame and window frames with wooden planks to conceal ourselves. I started to build modular square carrot crops inside the house in a grid to the left so we could generate income.
